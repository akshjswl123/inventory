PG Dump & Recreate

Run SQL, download a pg_dump backup, or recreate the app database from a dump file. The Node app renames the existing PGDATABASE (e.g. poc_backup_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS) if present, creates a fresh poc, restores the file, and reconnects its pool. Full inventory app
